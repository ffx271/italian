\section{Grammar: past progressive}
Not quite the same as the imperfect tense. Past progressive describes actions that were ongoing.
at a time in the past - often interrupted by something else happening. `While I was working (past
progressive), I received a call.'

To form the tense, \iti{stare} is used with the gerund, as shown in the table - note the \textbf{v}.
\begin{table}[h]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{r | l}
      \itb{io}      & \iti{stavo parlando}   \\
      \itb{tu}      & \iti{stavi parlando}   \\
      \itb{lui/lei} & \iti{stava parlando}   \\
      \itb{noi}     & \iti{stavamo parlando}   \\
      \itb{voi}     & \iti{stavate parlando}   \\
      \itb{loro}    & \iti{stavano parlando}   \\
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

For example, \iti{Mentre Luca stava mangiando, sua figlia lo chiamata.}
